## 2. Navigating "Today's Mission" (Daily Execution)

### 2.1 Why Tasks are Sequenced This Way
Every day, the recommendation engine allocates your time budget into an ordered, anti-passive sequence:
* **The Conjugation Floor:** At least 10–15 minutes of rapid verb conjugation drills on *Le Conjugueur* are mandatory from Day 1 to eliminate oral hesitation.
* **Spaced Repetition Retrieval (SRS):** 10–15 minutes of *Anki 5000 Most Common Words* with native audio to acquire vocabulary permanently.
* **Active Vocal Shadowing:** In A2 and B1, passive listening is replaced by reading transcripts aloud simultaneously with *InnerFrench* or *RFI* to build speech velocity and proper French breath groups.
* **Canadian Accent Calibration:** In B1/B2, regular Québécois listening modules (*Wandering French* & *Radio-Canada OHdio*) are scheduled to train your ear for Canadian test audio.

### 2.2 Using the Task Cards & Focus Timer
* **Task Titles:** Each card displays the explicit action (e.g., *"Mouth Anatomy & Nasal Vowel Drills"*), accompanied by the exact source resource (e.g., *Source: French Sounds*).
* **Launch Resource:** Click the **"Open"** button to jump directly to the verified external website, YouTube playlist, or Anki deck.
* **Focus Timer:** Click **"Timer"** to start a floating countdown sprint. When the timer hits 00:00, the task is automatically marked complete and your streak is updated.
* **Indian Learner Notes:** Look for the highlighted blue box on each card explaining how the specific grammar or sound maps to Hindi, Telugu, or English.

## 4. Indian Language Linguistic Bridge (How to Think in French)

1. **Grammatical Gender:**
   * French nouns are either Masculine (*un/le*) or Feminine (*une/la*).
   * **Hindi Rule:** Inanimate objects have gender in Hindi (*किताब f, कमra m*). Use your Hindi mental model to accept gendered objects naturally.
   * **Telugu Rule:** Telugu uses natural gender (Achethana is neuter). Do **not** think in Telugu neuter terms for French nouns; always memorize nouns with their articles (*la table, le livre*).
2. **Social Register (*Tu* vs *Vous*):**
   * *Tu* = **तू / तुम** (Hindi) = **నువ్వు** (Telugu). Used strictly for friends, family, and peers.
   * *Vous* = **आप** (Hindi) = **మీరు** (Telugu). Used for strangers, administrative clerks, examiners in Section A, and superiors.
3. **Nasal Vowels (*an/en, in/ain, on, un*):**
   * Exactly equivalent to the Hindi **Chandrabindu** (*माँ, आँख, चाँद*). Air escapes through both nose and mouth simultaneously without pronouncing the final "N" consonant.
4. **Sentence Syntax:**
   * French and English are strictly **SVO** (Subject - Verb - Object): *Je mange une pomme* / *I eat an apple*.
   * Hindi and Telugu are **SOV**: *मैं सेब खाता हूँ* / *నేను ఆపిల్ తింటున్నాను*. Do not put the main verb at the end in French!
5. **English Latin Cognates:**
   * ~35% of English vocabulary carries over directly: Convert *-tion* (Condition $\to$ Condition), *-ty* $\to$ *-té* (Liberty $\to$ Liberté), *-ly* $\to$ *-ment* (Rapidly $\to$ Rapidement).

## 5. TEF Canada vs. TCF Canada Strategy Vault

### 5.1 Official Government of Canada (IRCC) Score Requirements (CLB 7 / NCLC 7)
*Source: [Canada.ca Language Testing Criteria](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-requirements/language-testing.html)*

| Ability | **TEF Canada (CCIP)** - *Équivalence Ancien Score* | **TCF Canada (FEI)** - *Standard Score* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Compréhension de l'écrit (Reading)** | **207 – 232** / 300 (Min: **207**) | **453 – 498** / 699 (Min: **453**) |
| **Compréhension de l'oral (Listening)** | **249 – 279** / 360 (Min: **249**) | **458 – 502** / 699 (Min: **458**) |
| **Expression écrite (Writing)** | **310 – 348** / 450 (Min: **310**) | **10 – 11** / 20 (Min: **10**) |
| **Expression orale (Speaking)** | **310 – 348** / 450 (Min: **310**) | **10 – 11** / 20 (Min: **10**) |

> **⚠️ WARNING ON TEF FILING:** On your TEF Canada certificate, always use the **"Équivalence ancien score"** column for your Express Entry profile. Submitting numbers from the "Score / 699" column will lead to application rejection by IRCC.

### 5.2 Key Operational Differences
* **Listening Audio Playback:**
  * **TEF Canada:** Single-pass audio only (every audio track plays strictly once).
  * **TCF Canada:** Mixed playback (short audio plays once, longer dialogue plays twice).
* **Speaking (Expression Orale):**
  * **TEF Canada:** 2 sections (Section A: 5-min Inquiry using *VOUS* + Section B: 10-min Persuasion debate using *TU*).
  * **TCF Canada:** 3 tasks (Task 1: Self-intro, Task 2: Inquiry, Task 3: Spontaneous point of view presentation).
* **Writing Sandbox:**
  * Use the interactive **Live Writing Sandbox** in the Exam Hub tab with real-time word counting and one-click insertion of connectors (*En premier lieu, Force est de constater, Néanmoins, Par conséquent*).
